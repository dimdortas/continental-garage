Athens demands a hang-out place

The motor enthusiasts of Athens have long sought a home — a temple of sorts — to share their passion with the community.

Continental Garage is the destination to preserve and grow the culture of analogue fun, of motoring passion, of motorsports and of craftsmanship.

Everyone is welcome to share and create experiences.
